Getting started

yt-mirror keeps a folder on your Mac in sync with YouTube channels. You decide per channel whether to pick videos yourself or mirror everything.
1. Add a channel
Click "Add channel" and paste the channel link or @handle. yt-mirror reads the full video list.
2. Mirror videos
Tick the videos you want and click "Mirror". Or turn on "Auto-mirror" to get everything, including future uploads.
3. Exclude what you never want
"Exclude forever" hides a video from mirroring for good. Restore it any time from the Excluded tab.
4. Find your files
Each channel has its own folder. Files are named exactly like the video titles and dated with the upload time.

Good to know

  • Closing the window quits yt-mirror. Unfinished downloads continue the next time you open it.
  • Your videos are ordinary files. They keep working without yt-mirror.
  • Sort a channel folder by "Date Created" in Finder to see the same order as on YouTube.
