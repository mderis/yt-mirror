Your favourite YouTube channels, safely on your own disk

yt-mirror keeps a folder on your Mac in sync with the channels you choose. Pick videos or mirror everything, and skip the ones you never want.

Install in three steps

1. Open Terminal
Press Cmd + Space, type "Terminal" and press Enter.
2. Paste one line
Copy the line above, paste it and press Enter. Everything is set up inside one folder. No password needed.
3. Open yt-mirror
Click the yt-mirror icon in your Applications folder. You never need Terminal again, updates happen inside the app.
Prefer to see what the installer does first? Read the install script or install manually from GitHub.

What it does

Mirror a whole channel or pick videos
See every video with thumbnail, length and date. Download a selection, or turn on auto-mirror and get new uploads automatically.
Exclude videos forever
Hide the videos you never want, by hand or with simple rules such as a word in the title.
Clean files you can use anywhere
Each file is named exactly like the video title. Its date is the upload time, so Finder sorts a folder just like the channel.
Everything inside the file
Title, description, chapters, thumbnail and English subtitles are embedded in each video file.
Adopts what you already have
Point it at a folder of old downloads. It matches them to the channel and can fix names, dates and metadata without downloading again.
Looks after itself
Installs its own tools, updates itself in one click and explains problems in plain language.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. yt-mirror is open source under the MIT license.
Do I need a Google account?
No. An optional YouTube API key can make date loading faster. The key is free and needs no payment details, and the app works fully without it.
Where are my videos stored?
In a folder you choose, by default Movies/YouTube. They are normal video files that work without yt-mirror.
How do I update?
The app tells you when a new version exists and updates in one click. Your channels and videos are not touched.
How do I uninstall?
Settings, then "Uninstall yt-mirror". Your videos always stay.
Something is not working. Where do I get help?
Use "Report a problem" in the app. It prepares a debug report and opens GitHub Issues.
Does it work on Windows or Linux?
Not yet. Version 1 is for macOS only.
